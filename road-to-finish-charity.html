Road to Finish Charity

Tool(s) used: Adobe Illustrator

The Road to Finish Charity is a fictional organization dedicated to supporting individuals affected by cholesterol, cancer, and other health conditions. Through community marathons and charity events, the organization fosters unity among participants of all ages and backgrounds. The logo’s circular road design symbolizes the journey and perseverance inherent in running marathons, reflecting the endurance and excitement runners experience crossing the finish line. This design concept connects the mission of the charity to the physical and emotional strength athletes display during races, while emphasizing themes of hope, resilience, and community support.
