Nintendo Power Magazine Remake

Tool(s) used: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign

The Nintendo Magazine is arguably my most time consuming project due to the amount of requirements such as colour, typography, imagery, dimensions, etc. After researching the magazine, I used Illustrator to create the logo, images in the required dimensions, and transferred over to InDesign. With InDesign, I added the text, profile feature, mini-feature, featured video game, and cover story. The hardest challenge I faced here was the amount of ideas for an ad that were relevant in 2023 as that was the year the featured game I focused on was released. The overall magazine was 28 pages and once it was complete, and the feedback provided by my professor, I printed the entire magazine on a 8.5 x 11 paper and presented it in class. What I learned from creating my magazine was how to manage a large-scale project from start to finish, including research, design, and production. I gained valuable experience in researching the Nintendo Power magazine’s structure, including its dimensions, colours, typography, and imagery, to recreate its essence accurately.
