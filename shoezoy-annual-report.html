Shoezoy Annual Report

Tool(s) used: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign

The Shoezoy Annual Report 2023 project showcases my ability to translate comprehensive corporate information into a visually engaging and professional document. This report highlights Shoezoy’s decade-long commitment to innovation, quality, and sustainability in the footwear industry. Through clean layouts and structured presentation of key sections—such as company overview, management team, financial data, and strategic priorities—I aimed to deliver clarity and impact. The report emphasizes Shoezoy’s growth, inclusive culture, and dedication to customer satisfaction, reflecting the brand’s dynamic and forward-thinking identity. Designing this annual report allowed me to combine data visualization, typography, and branding principles to create a cohesive communication tool that supports Shoezoy’s corporate image and investor relations.
