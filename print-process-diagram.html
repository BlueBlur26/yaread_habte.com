Print Process Diagram

Tool(s) used: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator

The Print Process Diagram is a dynamic and visually captivating piece that outlines the ten essential steps in producing professional printed materials, all designed with a playful Sonic the Hedgehog theme. Using vibrant colors and diamond-shaped markers resembling rings from the Sonic games, the diagram guides viewers through each stage—from initial design creation and drafting, through client feedback and printer consultation, to prepress checks, finishing, and final delivery. The conveyor belt layout echoes the fast-paced energy of Sonic making the print process both engaging and easy to follow. Each step is clearly explained, highlighting important tasks like verifying color accuracy, trimming, folding, and applying special effects. This Sonic-themed print process diagram is an effective educational tool that adds fun and clarity to the complex workflow of print and publication design.
