Retro City Website

Tool(s) used: Visual Studio Code

Retro City is a tribute website dedicated to celebrating the golden age of video games. The project showcases a curated collection of classic games from legendary consoles such as the NES, SNES, and Sega Genesis. It aims to capture the nostalgic feel of retro gaming by highlighting iconic titles like Super Mario Bros., Sonic the Hedgehog, and The Legend of Zelda. The website serves as a digital museum and resource for both longtime gamers and newcomers who want to explore the roots and evolution of video gaming culture. Through engaging visuals and a user-friendly design, Retro City brings together timeless games and their stories, creating an immersive experience that honors the impact and legacy of classic video games.
