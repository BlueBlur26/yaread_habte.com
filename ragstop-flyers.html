Ragstop Flyers

Tool(s) used: Adobe Illustrator

My Ragstop flyers and internship project showcase a dynamic range of promotional materials designed to capture customer interest and drive sales. The flyers utilize vibrant colors, clear typography, and engaging imagery of popular products such as plush toys, gaming characters, and apparel to create a visually compelling narrative. Each flyer is tailored to highlight specific sales events or product categories, such as the Boxing Day sale and the plush toy discount, which effectively communicates value to the target audience. My internship experience at Ragstop demonstrates my ability to blend creative design principles with strategic marketing objectives, producing professional-quality materials that support brand identity and enhance customer engagement. Overall, my work reflects strong skills in visual communication, marketing strategy, and digital design, contributing meaningfully to Ragstop’s promotional efforts.
