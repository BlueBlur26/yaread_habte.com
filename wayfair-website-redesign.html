Wayfair Website Redesign

Tool(s) used: Adobe Photoshop

My Wayfair Website Redesign is a comprehensive project focused on creating a user-friendly and visually appealing online shopping experience. The design emphasizes intuitive navigation, with clear categories like Furniture, Outdoor, Bedding & Bath, and more, allowing users to find products quickly. The homepage features eye-catching promotional banners highlighting ongoing sales and featured items to engage visitors immediately. Product pages are designed to showcase items with detailed images, star ratings, pricing, and key information prominently displayed, encouraging confident purchasing decisions. Additionally, the footer provides easy access to customer service, company information, and social media links, ensuring users can find support and connect with the brand effortlessly. Overall, your redesign enhances usability, visual coherence, and accessibility, creating a modern and inviting online retail environment.
