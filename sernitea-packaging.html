Sernitea (2D and 3D Packaging)

Tool(s) used: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator

My Sernitea packaging project showcases a thoughtful blend of 2D and 3D design elements, tailored for print and publication purposes. The 2D designs highlight vibrant, nature-inspired graphics and clear nutritional information, emphasizing flavors such as Peach, Mango, and Mixed Berries in a visually appealing way. The 3D packaging mockups illustrate how these designs wrap around the tea boxes, creating an immersive and tangible product experience. The hand-drawn sketches demonstrate the ideation and layout planning process, ensuring that essential elements like product description, ingredients, instructions, and barcodes are strategically positioned for readability and aesthetic balance. Overall, this project successfully combines creative visuals with practical packaging considerations to deliver a cohesive and attractive product design for Sernitea.
