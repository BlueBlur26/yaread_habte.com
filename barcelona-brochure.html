Barcelona Brochure

Tool(s) used: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign

My digital illustration of the Barcelona Brochure captures the vibrant essence of this cosmopolitan city through a dynamic and colorful design. Using two distinct images, I skillfully showcase the architectural marvels and cultural richness of Barcelona, including iconic landmarks and bustling market scenes. The brochure’s layout balances informative text with visually engaging photos, making it both educational and appealing. My choice of typography and colour schemes reflects the city's lively atmosphere and historical depth, creating an inviting experience for viewers interested in exploring Barcelona's unique charm and artistic heritage. This project demonstrates my ability to blend imagery and content to communicate a compelling travel narrative effectively.
