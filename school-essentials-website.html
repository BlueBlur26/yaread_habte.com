School Essentials Website

Tool(s) used: Webflow

My School Essentials Website project, presented by Stonetile, was designed to be a comprehensive and user-friendly online resource for back-to-school supplies. I focused on creating a clean, organized layout that allows users—students, parents, and teachers—to easily navigate and find the products they need. Using Webflow, I was able to build an accessible and visually appealing website with clear typography and intuitive navigation. This project taught me the importance of balancing aesthetics with functionality to enhance the user experience. The goal was to help visitors quickly and efficiently gear up for a successful school year with affordable, quality essentials.
