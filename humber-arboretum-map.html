Humber Arboretum Map

Tool(s) used: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator

The Humber Arboretum Map is a detailed and visually clear guide designed to help visitors navigate the natural beauty and key landmarks of the arboretum. I focused on creating intuitive iconography and a clean layout that highlights essential features such as trails, entrances, exits, parking areas, and important safety warnings. The map legend includes universally recognizable symbols for fire hazards, recycling bins, water sources, and plant areas, making it easy for visitors to understand the environment and stay safe. Through thoughtful use of color and spatial organization, this map balances aesthetic appeal with practical functionality, providing a valuable tool for both casual visitors and nature enthusiasts exploring the Humber Arboretum.
