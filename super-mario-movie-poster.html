Super Mario Bros. Movie Poster

Tool(s) used: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator

This project involved redesigning the Super Mario Bros. movie poster with a fresh and creative approach. I focused on recreating the poster using my own custom typography to give it a unique and playful look that fits the iconic characters and theme of the film. The challenge was balancing the vibrant and whimsical feel of the Mario universe with clear, legible text that draws attention. Through this project, I enhanced my skills in digital illustration and type design while learning how to blend text and imagery harmoniously for impactful visual communication.
